Privacy Policy

App: Strong Password Generator

Strong Password Generator respects user privacy. This app is designed to generate strong passwords, customize password rules, and manage saved preferences directly on the user's device.

1. Information We Access

The app may use the following device features for user-initiated actions:

  • System clipboard, to copy a generated password when the user selects the copy action
  • System sharing, email, and external-link handlers, to perform user-initiated Share App, Our Apps, Rate Us, and Feedback actions

2. Information We Store

The app may store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • Password generator preferences
  • Password length and password count selections
  • Custom symbols preferences
  • Theme and appearance preferences
  • Other app personalization settings required for app functionality

This information is stored locally to support app functionality and personalization.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

At the current stage of the app, Strong Password Generator does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information through the app such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Payment information

The app is not designed as a social platform and does not provide public posting, public profiles, or public user content sharing.

4. How Data Is Used

Any locally stored data is used only to provide app features, including:

  • Restoring password generation settings and user preferences
  • Supporting password customization and regeneration flows
  • Allowing users to use copy, share, rating, and feedback-related convenience features
  • Preserving app personalization choices between sessions

5. Data Sharing

Strong Password Generator does not sell user data.

The app does not intentionally share personal data with third parties except where required to support core platform behavior or app functionality, such as:

  • Operating system clipboard handling for user-initiated copy actions
  • User-initiated external actions using installed device apps, email apps, or public store links

If future versions introduce analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, advertising, or third-party account features, this policy should be updated before release.

6. User Control

Users can control their data by:

  • Adjusting or resetting generator settings inside the app, where supported
  • Clearing the app's local storage by uninstalling the app or clearing app data from device settings
  • Choosing not to use copy, sharing, rating, or feedback features if they do not want to trigger those actions

7. Children's Privacy

Strong Password Generator is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed specifically to children.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep app data limited to what is required for the app's functionality. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes To This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, or release updates.

If the policy changes, the updated version should be published at the production privacy policy URL before or together with the related app release.
