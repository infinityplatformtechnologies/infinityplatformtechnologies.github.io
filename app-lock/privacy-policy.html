Privacy Policy

App: App Lock

App Lock respects user privacy. This app is designed to protect selected Android applications with a pattern or PIN and manage app-lock actions directly on the user's device.

1. Information We Access

The app may request access to:

  • Installed launchable app information, including app labels, package names, and icons, to allow users to choose which apps to protect
  • App usage events, to detect when a user-selected protected app enters the foreground
  • Display over other apps access, to show the lock screen above a user-selected protected app
  • Fingerprint authentication results provided by Android, when the user enables fingerprint unlock; the app does not access or store fingerprint templates

2. Information We Store

The app may store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • The master credential type, salt, and hashed credential value
  • The package names of apps selected for protection
  • Installed app labels, package names, system-app status, and cache timestamps used to load the app list efficiently
  • Basic app preferences, including fingerprint unlock and app-lock behavior settings

This information is stored locally to support app functionality and convenience.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

At the current stage of the app, App Lock does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Payment information

The app is not designed as a social platform and does not provide public posting, public profiles, or public user content sharing.

4. How Data Is Used

Any locally stored data is used only to provide app features, including:

  • Showing launchable apps that can be selected for protection
  • Restoring protected app selections and basic app state after reopening the app
  • Detecting when a user-selected protected app enters the foreground
  • Verifying the master pattern or PIN before allowing access
  • Allowing fingerprint authentication when the user enables fingerprint unlock

5. Data Sharing

App Lock does not sell user data.

The app does not intentionally share personal data with third parties except where required to support core platform behavior or app functionality, such as:

  • Operating system permission handling
  • Android system fingerprint authentication
  • User-initiated actions for sharing the app or opening external store and feedback links

The app does not use an account, backend, analytics service, or advertising service for app-lock protection. Credential data, protected app selections, installed app metadata, and app settings are not intentionally transmitted to the developer or third parties.

6. User Control

Users can control their data by:

  • Removing Usage Access or Display over other apps access from device settings
  • Adding or removing apps from the protected-app list
  • Disabling fingerprint unlock from the app settings
  • Clearing the app's local storage by uninstalling the app or clearing app data from device settings

7. Children's Privacy

App Lock is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed specifically to children.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep app data limited to what is required for the app's functionality. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes To This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, or release updates.

If the policy changes, the updated version should be published at the production privacy policy URL before or together with the related app release.
