Privacy Policy

App: Full Battery Alarm

Full Battery Alarm respects user privacy. This app is designed to monitor battery level, charging status, battery temperature, and battery details directly on the user's device.

1. Information We Access

The app may request access to:

  • Battery status information, to show battery percentage, charging status, health, voltage, current, technology, and temperature
  • Notification access, to allow the app to show battery alert notifications
  • Vibration access, to allow the app to vibrate when enabled battery alerts are triggered

2. Information We Store

The app may store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • Selected alert settings for full battery, low battery, and temperature alerts
  • Selected battery percentage and temperature threshold values
  • Sound, vibration, notification, repeat count, theme, and temperature unit preferences

This information is stored locally to support app functionality and convenience.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

At the current stage of the app, Full Battery Alarm does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Payment information

The app is not designed as a social platform and does not provide public posting, public profiles, or public user content sharing.

4. How Data Is Used

Any locally stored data is used only to provide app features, including:

  • Monitoring battery status and showing battery details inside the app
  • Restoring selected alert thresholds and preferences after reopening the app
  • Triggering full battery, low battery, and high temperature alerts based on user settings
  • Allowing users to share the app link or open the developer apps page through supported device actions

5. Data Sharing

Full Battery Alarm does not sell user data.

The app does not intentionally share personal data with third parties except where required to support core platform behavior or app functionality, such as:

  • Operating system permission handling
  • User-initiated sharing actions using installed device apps

If future versions introduce analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, advertising, or third-party account features, this policy should be updated before release.

6. User Control

Users can control their data by:

  • Disabling notification permission from device settings
  • Turning off sound, vibration, or alert settings inside the app
  • Clearing the app's local storage by uninstalling the app or clearing app data from device settings

7. Children's Privacy

Full Battery Alarm is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed specifically to children.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep app data limited to what is required for the app's functionality. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes To This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, or release updates.

If the policy changes, the updated version should be published at the production privacy policy URL before or together with the related app release.
